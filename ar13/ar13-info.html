Blood on the Clocktower

BotC is a social deduction game based on lying but also where everyone can participate even if they're dead! this means things like
  • dead people may speak at day
  • dead people have one vote (more on that later)
Another main characteristic of this game is character types! There are 5 character types that will be relevant this game: Townsfolk - "standard" good player - they have an ability which should conventionally help the good team. Outsiders - Good players who have abilities that hurt the good team. Minions - Evil players who have abilities that harm the good team. Demons - Evil players which usually kill people at night. The game immediately ends in a good win if there is no living demon. Travelers - good or evil (host decided) players who have strong abilities designed to help their team.

Voting Rules

voting in this game is very different (as opposed to mafia) All living players have one nomination. At any time, they may use this nomination to "open voting" on a player. Once a player is nominated, all players have a chance to vote on the nominated player. The player with the most votes (above the amount of living players divided by 2, rounded up)

Setup Specific Rules

This setup is called Trouble Brewing. For the playercount we are using, 5 townsfolk, 2 outsiders, 1 minion, and 1 demon will be in play, though these numbers can be modified. Note: we will have 1 traveler. This is the scapegoat. First come first serve on who gets it (https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scapegoat) This setup is bastard, in the sense that I can lie to you. the only way I can lie to you however, Is if you are drunk or poisoned. These have the same effect, where if you are drunk or poisoned, you have no ability, but you act as if you do; I. E. A drunk doctor would still choose someone, but they would not be protected.

Base Win Conditions

Once the demon is dead, the good team wins. Once only two players are left alive (one of which is the demon), the evil team wins.

Extra Win Conditions

to note, this script contains the Mayor, which states "If no execution occurs while only 3 players live, you win." since all teams win together, this is interchangable with "If no execution occurs while only 3 players live, the good team wins." this also means, if their condition triggers, the good team immediately wins and the evil team immediately loses.

Extra Loss Conditions

this script also contains the Saint, which states "If you die by execution, your team loses." since all teams win together, this means the good team would immediately lose and the evil team would immidiately win if the saint was executed. (edited)
