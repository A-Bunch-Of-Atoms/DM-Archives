Town of Salem 2 Anarchy Edition (25 Players)

Inspired by Town of Salem's "True All Any" this will be unbalanced. This will be somewhat like town of salem, but also in Discord Mafia formatting. So there's always some changes. ------- Re-roll criteria: 1) An unique role rolls more than once.
  • Town Power can only roll twice
-- If Town Power rolls more than twice, then it will be rerolled into Random Town
  • Godfather / Mafioso / Ambusher can only roll once each
-- If rolled again, it will be rerolled into Random mafia.
  • All Coven and Acolytes of the Apocalypse can only roll once each
-- Coven will reroll into another Coven if applicable, and Apocalypse will be rerolled into Any
  • Pirate will also be rerolled into Any. 2) Factional limit of 4 is reached.
  • 1 Coven Power may roll (Coven leader, Hex Master, Witch)
  • If Godfather does not roll naturally, and it be rolled in as long as 2 Mafia rolled. -------- Notes: 1) All roles except for the Mafia will be from TOS 2, some do have slight re-works to work with Discord Mafia formatting.. 2) Conversion roles (Cultist, Vampires, Cursed Souls, ect.), and roles with no abilities are banned (example Pilgrim). 3) Neutral Apocalypse if solo, will have basic defense and appear innocent to the sheriff. 4) Mafia will be able to multitask their factional kill, however, this will be seen as them visiting 2 targets if they target two different people. 5) There will be Last Wills and Death Notes see the section below for more information.
