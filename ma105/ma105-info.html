MA105 - Reach Out To The Truth

An 18p semi-open setup ran by @sunny. Mysterious serial murders are sending waves through a quiet, foggy town... Setup - 18p + Town Citizen x9 + Random Town PR x2 + Mason x2 - Mafia Goon x2 - Random Mafia PR x2 = Jack-of-all-trades Serial Killer The random roles will be determined by randomly selecting a knight move in chess on a given 4x4 table. If you'd like to learn more, click here. Changes to original setup:
- Instead of having a default 1-shot Strongman, the Serial Killer gets a default 1-shot bulletproof that protects them from the first kill against them. - If they would get another BPV due to a Bulletproof being in the game, they get the 1-shot strongman instead. - The Serial Killer automatically wins in a 1v1v1. - Gunsmith can't detect the Serial Killer, but can detect the Godfather. - Cops can detect the Serial Killer, but cannot detect the Godfather (or SK using godfather) - Jailkeeper is non-consecutive, as well as the doctor. - Town Bulletproof is 1-shot, like the SK. Mafia bulletproof is not. Factional kill is multitaskable.

Roles

1. Vanilla Townie/Mafia Goon - The base roles with no abilities. 2. Cop: Target a player and get results as: "Mafia", "Not Mafia", "No Result" 3. Gunsmith: Each night, target a player and receive results as "Has a Gun", "Does not have a Gun". Mafia and the Cop will show as having a gun. (edited)
1:56 PM
4. Tracker: Each night, target another player and receive results as: "No Visit", "Visited X", "Visited X and Y" (for a mafia multitasking) 5. Even-Night Watcher: On even nights, choose a player and learn the names of all who visit that player that night. 6. Doctor: Each night, target a player and protect them from a single attack. You cannot selftarget, nor you can you target the same player twice in a row. 7. 1-Shot Bulletproof: Once, when attacked, you will survive. 8. Roleblocker: Each night, target a player, that player's night action will fail that night. If that action was using a charge, the charge will be lost. (This does not block passives, such as the Bulletproof.) 9. Jailkeeper: Each night protect your target from one attack and roleblock your target. You cannot target the same player twice in a row. 10. 3-Shot Commuter: Thrice, become unaffected by any night actions that night. 11. Godfather: A member of the Mafia that will appear as "Not Mafia" to the Cop. 12. Mason: You start knowing another player is Town and may speak in a private chat with them.

Game Mechanics

Day: 48 hours Night: 24 hours Mafia Chat: Available 24/7 Mason Chat: Available 24/7 Factional Kill: Mandatory. Multitasking: Allowed Kill Credit: Disabled Feedback: Feedbackless/No Feedback

Voting Mechanics

Plurality: Enabled Majority: Enabled Day 2 No Lynch: Disabled Ties: RNG No Votes: RNG Flips: Full Flips

Activity Requirements

You are required to post at least 25 times each 24 hours, otherwise you'll receive a prod. If you don't post once in a 24h period, you'll receive 2 prods instead. If you receive 3 prods, you'll be replaced and potentially sign-up banned from future games. Please only sign up for this game if you are able to commit an good amount of time to it! ### Other Info
