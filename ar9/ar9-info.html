Welcome to UNO!

The well-known card game that I’m sure all of you have played. If not, I will go over the rules.

Rules:

Each player gets 7 cards to start. 1 card is placed in the discard pile to start the game. To play a card, it must match either the number or the color of the top card in the discard pile. If you cannot play a card you must draw exactly 1 card from the draw pile, if you can play it, then you may play it, otherwise your turn is skipped. You have 24 hours to submit your turn, if you do not submit your turn in time it will be skipped. There are also action cards, which are:
  • Skip: Skip the next player’s turn.
  • Draw 2: The next player draws 2 cards.
  • Reverse: Reverses the turn order.
  • Wild: Change the color to any other color. Can be played on any color.
  • Draw 4: The next player draws 4 cards, and you change the color to any other color. Can be played on any color. When you have exactly one card left be the first to call UNO! or else another player will make you have to draw 2 ## House Rules:
When there is a Draw 2 played against you, if you have a Draw 2 you may play it to make the next player draw 4, this may repeat until somebody doesn't have a Draw 2 to place. This also applies to Draw 4's (But note draw 2's and draw 4's cant stack on each other, only themselves)

Win Conditions

The first player to run out of cards win the round every other player gets the sum of their cards as points, whomever has the least points by the end of 3 rounds wins -# Note: if there is a tie at the end of round 3 there will be a tiebreaker round and whoever wins that is the winner

Point Values

1-10: Their face value Draw 2, Skip, Reverse: 20 points Wild, Draw 4: 50 points

Turn Order

Turn order is randomized by secret sources (OoOoOoO) and will stay the same unless a reverse card is used. JOIN THE FOLLOWING SERVER IN ORDER TO PLAY: https://discord.gg/uzYc9yctFu
