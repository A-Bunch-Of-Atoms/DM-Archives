@Alive Line EX I noticed that there was a missing explanation on how illusions are handled when the Illusionist dies. The wiki now features the following text in the Illusionists' Role Interactions.
If there is an illusion currently placed when the Illusionist dies, or if the Illusionist places one during the night he dies, then the illusion will be removed starting the phase after the illusionists death, but may still trigger during the phase of his death and that player will flip as an Illusionist alongside the real illusionist if that player were to die that same phase.
