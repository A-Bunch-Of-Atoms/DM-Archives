Welcome to UNO! No Mercy.

A spin on the well-known card game UNO with more intense rules.

Rules:

Each player gets 7 cards to start. 1 card is placed in the discard pile to start the game. -# if this card is an action card, that card will act as if it were played on the first player (if its a skip, the first player is skipped), this does not apply to 7's and 0's To play a card, it must match either the number or the color of the top card in the discard pile. -# or be the same action card You may not draw when you are able to play. When you are unable to play a card, you must draw cards until you are able to play a card.

No Mercy Special Rules:

Stacking – If someone plays a Draw Card (+2, +4, +6, +10), you can “Stack” the penalty by playing a Draw Card from your hand of equal or higher value. If you do, the card you played adds to the total penalty for the next player. Mercy – If a player ever has 25 or more cards in their hand, they are out of the game. 7’s Swap – When you play a 7 card of any color, you MUST swap your hand with another player of your choice. 0’s Pass – When you play a 0 card of any color, ALL players must pass their hand to the next player in the current direction of play. There are also action cards, which are:
3:43 AM
  • Skip: Skip the next player’s turn.
  • Draw 2: The next player draws 2 cards.
  • Draw 4: The next player draws 4 cards. This is not a wild.
  • Reverse: Reverses the turn order.
  • Discard All: Discard all cards in your hand that match the color of this card.
  • Skip All: Skip everyone's turn and go again.
  • Wild Reverse Draw 4: Change the color to any other color. Can be played on any color. Reverse the turn order to cause the person that played before you to draw 4.
  • Wild Draw 6: Change the color to any other color. Can be played on any color. The next player has to draw 6.
  • Wild Draw 10: Change the color to any other color. Can be played on any color. The next player has to draw 10.
  • Wild Color Roulette: Change the color to any other color. Can be played on any color. The next player has to draw until they get the chosen color. When you have exactly one card left, be the first to call UNO! in the uno channel or else another player will make you draw 2.

    Win Conditions

    The first player to run out of cards wins the round.
Every other player gets the sum of their cards as points. Whoever has the least points by the end of 3 rounds wins.

Point Values

  • 0-9: Their face value
  • Draw 2, Draw 4, Skip, Skip All, Reverse: 20 points
  • Wild Reverse Draw 4, Wild Draw 6, Wild Draw 10, Wild Color Roulette: 50 points

Turn Order

Turn order is randomized and will stay the same during a round unless a reverse card is used.

Activity

You must play your turn in 24 hours to not get prodded. If you do not play, you will get 24 more hours. If you do not play in this extended amount of time, you will get 2 prods, and your turn will be skipped. If you only have one card that is able to be played and it will not leave you with one card after playing it, you will be autoplayed. You may beforehand set up autoactions. -# Hosts are not responsible for your autoaction turning out badly. -# rules may be modified at host discretion (edited)
