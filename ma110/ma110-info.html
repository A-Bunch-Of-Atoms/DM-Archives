~ ~ ~ Welcome to MA110: Sharing Is Caring ~ ~ ~

Hosted by @Trapped (Love Of Nge’s Life) and @Ngekop, it’s an open setup 13p (10 Towns vs 3 mafias). All town power roles share 4 combined shots and all mafia share 5 combined shots. The following will be the rules, mechanics, as well as the setup.

Setup

- Town X-shot Cop - Town X-shot Jailkeeper - Town X-shot Vigilante - 7 Citizens - Mafia Y-shot Rolecop - Mafia Y-shot Doctor - Mafia Y-shot Framer
  • "X" starts as 4, and decreases with each shot spent by one of the power roles.
  • "Y" starts as 5, and decreases with each shot spent by one of the power roles.
  • If X or Y goes below 0 in one night, all actions by that team will fail and no shots will be spent.
  • Every time a Town Power Role dies, X increases by 1.
  • Every time a Mafia Member dies, Y increases by 1.
