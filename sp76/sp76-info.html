Gnosia Hunt Setup Gnosia Hunt is a setup designed for 15 players and inspired by the game Gnosia. Setup (15 Players) Crew (10x): 1x Engineer 1x Scientist 1x Angel 2x Guard Duty 5x Crewmate Gnos (4x): 3x Gnosia 1x AC Follower Neutral (1x): 1x Bug Game Mechanics Each day, all players will vote on who shall be placed into Cold Sleep. A player in Cold Sleep is eliminated from the game, but can be checked by the Scientist. In the event of a tie vote at the end of the day, the day will extend by 24 hours. During the day's second half, only players who ended the first half tied for the most votes can be voted for. All players, both those eligible to be voted and those ineligible, may participate in the second half of the discussion and voting. Additionally, a new option is added. Freeze All. If this option is selected, all players from this second half are placed into Cold Sleep rather than only one. Freeze All can only be used if the option has a Majority of votes at the end of the day. If Freeze All lacks a Majority of votes at the end of the day, every player whose final vote was Freeze All has their vote changed to Abstain and then Plurality is used to determine the vote. If two or more players are still tied at the end of the day, no one is placed into Cold Sleep and the day ends. If Freeze All would freeze half or more of the remaining players, it does not become an available option to vote for. Each night, the Gnosia target one player and attempt to eliminate them. If they succeed, their target Disappears and cannot be checked by the Scientist. The Gnosia win when half or more of the surviving players are Gnosia, or the Crew wins when all the Gnosia are placed into Cold Sleep. The AC Follower does not count as Gnosia for this purpose. If the Bug has not Disappeared or been placed into Cold Sleep when the game ends, the Bug wins and all others players lose, overriding all other win conditions.
8:56 PM
Role Assignment At the start of the game, everyone will be secretly assigned a role. Any player whose role is Gnosia, AC Follower, Bug, or Guard Duty immediately learns their role. All other players only learn they are Crew, and do not learn their specific role. When Night One starts, all Crew will learn their specific role. Day Skills and Statistics In addition to a night role, each player possesses 6 Day Skills. 24 hours after the start of the Day Phase, it is announced which players successfully used which Day Skills on which targets and they then take effect. If there is a tie vote and the day continues, any players who did not successfully use a Day Skill in the day's first half may attempt to use one in the second half. Each player can only use one Day Skill per day. Once a player successfully uses a Skill, they cannot use that Skill again for the rest of the game. Successfully used Skills take effect in the order listed below. If multiple players would successfully use the same Skill, the order in which they take effect is randomly determined, possibly causing some of them to fail, depending on the Skill. Each player also has six Statistics which determine the success chance of their Day Skills. The total of a player's Statistics is always 250%. A given statistic cannot be less than 1% or more than 99%. Fifteen possible sets of Statistics are created before the game and each player is randomly assigned one of the fifteen sets once the game begins. Statistic assignment and Role/Faction assignment are completely separate.
