S&S

"A 5v5 team game with similarities to 'coup' and 'bloodbound' where the two teams aim to eliminate each other's leader through psychological deception, strategy and guesswork"

setup

  • at the start of game, teams and player order are decided on
  • there is a deck of 10 cards for each team, consisting of 4 swords, 3 shields, 2 bare hands and 1 double wield
  • each player has a left hand and a right hand slot to equip cards # aim
eliminate the opposing team's leader

starting phase

Some things to be decided on before the main gameplay
  • teams decide a leader in secret
  • teams also decide to allocate their cards to players slots to equip (facedown/unseen)
  • some strategy talk before the game # attacking phase
the main phase where actions alternate between the teams (team A plays, then team B then A...) and turns rotate through the players of a team (after a turn, players have to wait for all their remaining teammates go before having another turn)
  • on a player's turn they can pass (do nothing) or attack
  • when attacking, you claim to 'use [a card type (sword or double wield)] on my left/right slot to attack [player]'.
  • swords deal 1 hit and double wield deals 2 hits
  • since you 'claim' to attack you can lie about the actual equipped card in that slot # defending
when you have been targeted you may 'defend with a shield (can be fake too) in my left/right slot'
  • every slot can withstand 3 hits before being unable to protect, but can still attack
  • if you cannot defend anymore then you are eliminated* # challenging
when a player on the opposing team claims to attack/defend with X slot you may challenge their action (think they are lying) and reveal the card in X slot.
  • if the card is what they are claimed, you will be eliminated and the action still continues through*
  • if the cardis not what they claimed, they will be eliminated and their turn is passed* *eliminated players do not reveal their unrevealed cards

Team selection

Usually the teams are selected, starting with 2 leaders and a coin toss/rps or some other way to decide who picks first, then going through 1-2-2-2-1 player picks As a game online I will use random rolling because you guys don't know everyone else all. If you do want this selection get at least 6 ppl to agree then we can make it happen.

Team play

  • Just ping me (or any hosts later) in the ar10 chat or in private channel to play your move 'Puppetting' or 'puppeting' (idk if that's the term and how u spell it) is where you can decide and play a actions for teammates. It will be active in this game.
  • you can lock out 2 teammates max from being able to puppet you (gotta send your lock out's in player chat).
  • if multiple players send something before I check then I will prioritise the player first then whoever sent the last action that can puppet said player # Prods
  • if a whole team sits on a turn for 24h then every member who can puppet/play will get 1 warning while the player to do an action will get an additional prod
  • if a whole team sits on a possible challenge for 24h then every member will just get a warning and there will be no challenge
  • after 2 warnings, each warning will be a prod instead
  • force replacement after 3 prods # Clarifications:
  • You may challenge anybody from the other team any time an action involving a card slot is made (even revealed ones)
  • Turns are only passed for people who are eliminated by a challenge for their attack phase, not defending
  • Group 1 starts the game so Vee will be 1st up
  • What defends an attack is the slot, it can defend at max 3 hits or until it is revealed as a non-shield card
  • Double hits from dual wield can not be transferred over 2 slots
  • After being eliminated, you can only talk in game chat and not player chats anymore
