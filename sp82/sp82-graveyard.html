    • If you were to be voted out, the player with the next highest amount of votes is voted out instead, RNG if tied.

Antonio's Abilities

  • [1x] Confinement: Once, at night, if Axetonio is alive, confine another player by attacking them, if they would die, you will instead confine them and Axetonio will hold the keys to their cell.
    • The confined player survives but enters the confinement state, which will cause the following.
      • They cannot use any of their role abilities or attribute/passives.
      • They will be announced as Confined by Antonio, Leader of the Warlocks the next day.
      • They may continue to talk and vote and they can still be voted out during the day, if this occurs, they still die.
      • If Axetonio, Executioner of the Warlocks dies then the keys will be uncovered, freeing the confined player.
      • If the confined player isn't freed within 6 phases, they will unconditionally die.
  • [Doom]: Each night you may visit another player and afflict them with Doom.
    • Once half the living players (excluding coven) are afflicted with Doom, an announcement is posted at the next applicable SOD: "Antonio, Leader of the Warlocks is halfway done with his ritual!"
    • Once every living non-coven player is afflicted with Doom, an announcement is posted at the next applicable SOD: "Antonio, Leader of the Warlocks has completed his ritual, if he is not stopped during this day, the Coven will be victorious."
      • If you are not voted out or otherwise die on that day phase you will cause a Coven victory, irregardless of game state. Anyone who would win alongside the coven also wins, any other major faction as well as 3Ps are considered to have lost (so roles winning on a certain faction besides the coven losing will also win).

Necronomicon

If alive Axetonio, Leader of the Warlocks will receive the Necronomicon before you do and will be the first to receive it. If dead, you will receive it first and will gain basic defense, detection immunity and your Doom ability also attacks your target. -# This role was originally designed for CO25 - Warlock Occupation.
